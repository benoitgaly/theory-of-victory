Where this figure comes from

Foreign support to Ukraine

The sources

  1. Chart “Aid allocations by donor group”, 2022–June 2026, inflation-adjusted monthly averages, in billions of euros

    Kiel Institute for the World Economy · Chart reading · The file name carries its publication date: 13 August 2026 · read on 14 August 2026

    This is the chart file itself, served by the publisher, and its name carries the publication date — an address that cannot be silently replaced by a later version. The values below are READ OFF IT BY EYE, to ±0.05 €bn/month: this is not an extraction from the dataset. The chart also measures ALLOCATION — what is assigned to a concrete package — and not delivery; that is one rung above what the engine represents, and the whole 2024 discrepancy comes from there.

  2. Ukraine Support Tracker — Release 30, sheet “Fig A31 Monthly Avg 2022-2026” (Monthly Average of Aid Allocations by Donor Group: 2022–June 2026, billion Euros)

    Kiel Institute for the World Economy · Published measurement · Release 30, covering 24 January 2022 to 30 June 2026 · read on 14 August 2026

    This is the dataset UNDERNEATH the “Aid allocations by donor group” chart read off by eye until now: same three groups, same two types, same five periods. The values are now read in the cell, no longer off a bar height. Structure of the table: rows 12 to 16 = 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026; columns E, F, G, H = military (United States, Europe, Other donors, Total); columns I, J, K, L = financial and humanitarian (the same four). The formulas sum column Q of the bilateral database — “tot_sub_activity_value_constant_currency” — hence CONSTANT EUROS, adjusted for inflation and exchange rates, 2021 base (the Country_Deflator sheet writes “Currency Power Change (2021 = baseline)”). Explicit filters in the formula: measure = allocation, type = Military or Financial & Humanitarian, month within the bounds of the year. “Europe” = EU member states and institutions, Norway, United Kingdom, Iceland, Switzerland. “Other donors” = Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand, South Korea, Türkiye, China, Taiwan, India. The sheet says nothing about what was delivered: allocation only.

  3. Ukraine Support Tracker: Europe leads on aid – US weapons remain key

    Kiel Institute for the World Economy · Published measurement · Page dated 13 August 2026 by its publisher · read on 14 August 2026

    The press release that accompanies the chart, used here as a CONTROL on the reading of the chart. It states in words a value the chart gives only as a bar height: “financial and humanitarian aid was 41 percent lower than in 2025” as a monthly average. The reading gives 1.77 against 3.01, i.e. −41.2%: it is confirmed to a tenth of a point. The release also quantifies the European loan — close to €11bn allocated over May and June 2026, of which 4.3 military and 6.7 financial, under a €90bn Ukraine Support Loan for 2026-2027 — and states that European donors bought at least €3bn of armaments from American industry in the first half of 2026.

  4. Ukraine Support Tracker — Release 30, sheet “Fig 2. Country Groups (stacked)” (Aid allocations and residual commitments across donor groups, billion Euros)

    Kiel Institute for the World Economy · Published measurement · Release 30, cumulative from 24 January 2022 to 30 June 2026 · read on 14 August 2026

    The sheet publishes side by side, for geographic Europe and for the United States, the cumulative COMMITTED and ALLOCATED, and the gap between the two. It also gives the definition the tracker uses: “Allocations are defined as aid which has been delivered OR SPECIFIED FOR DELIVERY” — an allocation therefore includes equipment earmarked but not yet on its way. That is precisely why allocation is not delivery. Amounts in CURRENT euros (the formulas go back to columns M and P of the database, undeflated), unlike the Fig A31 sheet. The sheet does not quantify the “Other donors” group.

  5. Ukraine Support Tracker — Release 30, sheets “Aggregates by Country Group” and “Fig A22. Cumulative Alloc.”

    Kiel Institute for the World Economy · Published measurement · Release 30, cumulative from 24 January 2022 to 30 June 2026 · read on 14 August 2026

    “Aggregates by Country Group” breaks commitments and allocations down by country and by sub-group (EU, Anglo-Saxon countries, other donor countries, geographic Europe), in billions of current euros, across the three types of aid. “Fig A22” gives the cumulative monthly series and its end point at 30 June 2026. These sheets do not follow the group split of Fig A31: the “Other donors” total in the sense of the chart has to be rebuilt by adding Canada, Australia, New Zealand and the “Other donor countries” block. That addition is done here and flagged as such. Known discrepancy between the two sheets — €179.80bn against €179.34bn of military aid allocated — because the monthly series excludes allocations with no month assigned.

  6. Ukraine Support Tracker — Release 30, sheets “Fig 9. Budget Support by Donor” and “Financial disb per Month (€)” (Foreign budgetary support: allocations vs. disbursements)

    Kiel Institute for the World Economy · Published measurement · Release 30, from 24 January 2022 to 30 June 2026 · read on 14 August 2026

    THE source on actual disbursement, and the only one that gives amounts for it. It covers a single category: budgetary support to the Ukrainian state — loans, grants and guarantees paid into the budget. The disbursements do not come from the tracker but from the UKRAINIAN MINISTRY OF FINANCE, that is, from the recipient, which makes them a control independent of the donor. The sheet specifies “nominal value”: CURRENT euros, not adjusted for inflation. It proves nothing about military aid, nothing about humanitarian aid, and nothing about what the weapons that actually arrived are worth.

  7. Ukraine Support Tracker — Release 30, sheets “In-kind Military per Country”, “Fig A4. Weapon Delivery” and “Delivery Transparency Index”

    Kiel Institute for the World Economy · Published measurement · Release 30, from 24 January 2022 to 30 June 2026 · read on 14 August 2026

    The second and last window the tracker offers on what was delivered. It is in NUMBERS OF UNITS, never in euros, and it is limited to five categories of heavy weapons: 155/152 mm howitzers, multiple rocket launchers, tanks, surface-to-air systems, infantry fighting vehicles. The sheet publishes a “delivery rate” by category and by country, measured against the COMMITTED (“Committed / Delivered” columns), not against the allocated — a distinction that forbids mapping those rates back onto the allocated amounts. Nothing there covers ammunition, drones, spare parts or maintenance.

  8. Ukraine Support Tracker — Release 30, sheet “Bilateral Assistance, MAIN DATA” (line-by-line database, 5,820 activities, column “tot_value_deliv_EUR”)

    Kiel Institute for the World Economy · Published measurement · Release 30, from 24 January 2022 to 30 June 2026 · read on 14 August 2026

    The raw database behind every figure sheet, 80 columns. It carries a “tot_value_deliv_EUR” column — the euro value of what has been delivered — and counting it is what measures the exact size of the gap: the field is almost always empty. The fill count reported below is a count performed here on this database, not a figure published by the publisher, and it is flagged as such.
